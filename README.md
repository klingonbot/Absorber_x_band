# Metamaterial Absorber for X-band Application

A single-layer, low-profile wideband metamaterial absorber combining an outer ring resonator with a diamond-shaped patch structure, achieving >93% absorption across the 7.87–10.10 GHz frequency range.

## Overview

This project presents a metasurface-based electromagnetic absorber designed for X-band operation (8–10 GHz). The unit cell integrates an outer ring resonator with a diamond-shaped patch and four lumped resistors, backed by a continuous metallic ground plane. The design achieves strong impedance matching to free space, resulting in high absorption over a wide bandwidth while maintaining a low electrical thickness of approximately λ₀/21.

## Design Objectives

- Operating frequency: 8–10 GHz
- Absorption ≥ 90% across the operating band
- Thickness ≤ λ/10

## Key Results

- **Absorption:** >90% from 7.87 GHz to 10.10 GHz, with a minimum of 93% within the operating band
- **Electrical thickness:** ~λ₀/21 at 9 GHz (physical thickness 1.6 mm)
- **Polarization insensitivity:** Fourfold symmetry gives similar response for TE- and TM-polarized incidence
- **Angular stability:** Stable absorption performance for oblique incidence up to ±20°
